  • Buying or restoring Puphii Pro involves Apple (payment) and RevenueCat (subscription validation). RevenueCat does not receive your puff logs, skips or Voice Pep Rally audio.

1. Who we are

Puphii is offered by an individual developer based in Japan (“we”, “us”, “our”). For questions or privacy requests, contact puphiicount@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

  • The Puphii mobile app for iPhone (sections 3 to 8). Available through the App Store in the United States, Australia and New Zealand. There is no Android version in this release.
  • The Puphii marketing website at https://puphii.com (section 9).
  • This legal-documents site (section 10).

Sections 11 to 19 apply as relevant.

The marketing site does not currently collect email addresses through a connected signup. If an email signup is connected later, this policy will be updated before that goes live.

3. The app: information that stays on your device

Puphii works without an account. Habit-tracking content you enter is stored on your device. Puphii does not send that content to us or to advertising or analytics companies: there is no habit-data account, no cloud sync of logs, and no advertising or product-analytics SDK in the current design.

Puff and skip logs can reveal smoking or vaping habits and timing. That is why they are designed not to leave your device via Puphii.

Backups. On iPhone, this information is designed to be excluded from iCloud and computer backups. Deleting Puphii deletes it, and it does not move to a new device via an ordinary backup restore.

Information from Apple

Apple distributes the app. Apple’s handling of your App Store account, downloads and purchases is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy. Apple may provide developers with aggregate statistics (downloads, sales, page views) and, if you opt in on your device, crash or usage reports under Apple’s rules.

4. Deleting app data

In Settings → Data Management:

  • Clear puff history permanently deletes puff logs and related plan milestones and puff-free day confirmations as implemented. Plan settings, skips, savings details, Bubble Break scores and Voice Pep Rally library contents remain.
  • Reset everything clears logs, skips, plan, savings, Bubble Break and settings (reminders off), and deletes Voice Pep Rally recordings managed by the app, keeping only minimal technical records such as the date the app was first opened.
  • Deleting the app removes everything Puphii stored on your device.

We don’t hold a copy of this on-device information, so we can’t recover it after deletion.

5. Reminders

Daily reminders are optional. Puphii asks for notification permission only when you turn reminders on. Reminders are scheduled on your device; reminder content is not sent to our servers. Reminder text does not instruct you to smoke or vape.

6. Puphii Pro subscriptions

Puphii Pro subscriptions are sold and charged by Apple through your Apple Account. Puphii never sees your card or payment details.

There is no permanent free tier for normal use: an active subscription or introductory trial unlocks the same full app for every plan. Prices, periods and any free trial are shown by the store before you buy. Configured targets (must match App Store Connect): Annual — typically yearly pricing with a 7-day trial when eligible; Monthly — typically monthly pricing with a 3-day trial when eligible; Weekly — typically weekly pricing with no trial. See also the Terms of Use, section 5.

Puphii uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., United States) to check entitlement, show prices and trial eligibility, and restore purchases. The app connects to RevenueCat when needed for those purposes (including when checking entitlement).

What RevenueCat receives

  • A random RevenueCat customer ID (not linked by us to your name or email).
  • Your device’s identifier for vendor (IDFV).
  • Device and app details: device model, iOS version, Puphii version, preferred languages and your App Store country.
  • Purchase information from Apple needed to validate the subscription.
  • App Tracking Transparency status (Puphii never asks for tracking permission).
  • Your IP address, as with any internet connection. RevenueCat documents using the IP address to estimate country and not retaining the IP itself.
  • Technical information about RevenueCat’s software.

These identifiers do not include your name or contact details. They are persistent, so the information is pseudonymous, not anonymous: purchase history can be seen in RevenueCat’s dashboard linked to those IDs.

What RevenueCat does not receive

  • Your puff logs, skips, plan, savings details, settings, Bubble Break scores or Voice Pep Rally recordings.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Puphii does not use RevenueCat’s hosted paywall templates, advertising or attribution products.

Why and where

Only to provide the subscription: show what can be bought, confirm what you bought, and give you access. RevenueCat stores this information on Amazon Web Services servers in the United States.

Cancelling, restoring and deleting subscription records

Cancel in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. See Apple’s guide, Cancel a subscription from Apple. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Restore Purchases restores access to an active subscription bought with the same Apple Account. It does not restore on-device habit or Pep Rally data.

Deleting Puphii removes the customer ID from your device, but not the records RevenueCat holds. To ask us to delete records we can locate, email puphiicount@gmail.com. If you bought a subscription, include the order ID from your Apple receipt and the approximate purchase date. Non-subscriber records are usually not findable. Please don’t send card details, passwords or Apple Account sign-in details. Deleting these records does not cancel an active Apple subscription.

7. Links to other websites and services

The app’s Learn section may open public health pages (including cdc.gov, smokefree.gov and nhs.uk as currently configured). Links open only when you tap them.

Quick support / I Need Help may show quit, crisis and emergency contacts labeled by country for the United States, Australia and New Zealand. You choose which country’s list to view. The app does not use device geolocation. Destinations receive a normal browser or dialer request under their own practices.

Those links are not emergency monitoring or medical treatment by Puphii.

8. No advertising or tracking SDKs

Puphii has no advertising, attribution or crash-reporting SDKs, does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising, and does not sell personal information.

9. Marketing website

The Puphii marketing website at https://puphii.com is served through Cloudflare and built as a Canva website. Visiting it can involve:

  • standard web requests (such as IP address, user agent and requested paths);
  • Cloudflare security or performance cookies;
  • Canva’s hosting scripts and assets needed to render the page.

We have not added separate advertising pixels or cross-site marketing analytics of our own on that page. This is not a claim that Canva or Cloudflare are cookie-free.

If forms or email collection are enabled on the marketing site later, this section will be updated first.

11. How we use information

  • On-device data: to operate the features you use on that device.
  • Subscription data (via Apple / RevenueCat): to sell, validate and restore Puphii Pro.
  • Support email: to respond to your request.
  • Website and legal-site technical data: to operate and secure those sites.

We do not use on-device habit or Pep Rally content for advertising or profiling.

12. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information only as needed with:

  • Apple (App Store, payments, distribution);
  • RevenueCat (subscription validation), as described above;
  • hosting providers for the marketing website (Canva / Cloudflare) and for this site (GitHub);
  • authorities when required by law.

13. Retention

  • On-device data: until you delete it or delete the app.
  • RevenueCat subscription records: until you successfully request deletion we can action (subject to RevenueCat’s documented legal exceptions), or as needed to provide the subscription.
  • Support emails: as long as needed to handle your request and related legal obligations.
  • Provider logs: under each provider’s own practices.

14. Security

We design the app so sensitive habit content stays on the device. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure. Protect your device with a passcode and keep iOS updated.

15. Regional privacy notes and requests

Because the operator is based in Japan, Japan’s Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI) may apply to personal information we handle (for example support email contents or RevenueCat records we can locate). Contact puphiicount@gmail.com for access, correction or deletion requests we can action.

  • Australia: you may have rights under the Australian Privacy Principles. You can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
  • New Zealand: you may have rights under the Privacy Act 2020. You can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.
  • United States: depending on your state, you may have additional privacy rights. Contact us at the email above. We do not sell personal information.
  • Japan: the Personal Information Protection Commission.

16. Children

Puphii does not check ages in the app. The App Store content rating is assigned by Apple from its questionnaire and is separate from any contractual minimum age that may appear in the Terms of Use.

The app is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children as defined under applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us by email, contact puphiicount@gmail.com.

17. Educational scope

Puphii is an educational and habit-awareness tool. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Quick support and linked crisis resources are not a substitute for emergency services or clinical care.
