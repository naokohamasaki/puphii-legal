3. What Puphii is, and isn’t

Puphii is a habit-awareness companion. With an active subscription or introductory trial, you can:

  • log puffs and skips and see playful on-device reactions;
  • view insights, history and spending estimates;
  • set an optional reduction plan;
  • play Bubble Break;
  • record or import optional Voice Pep Rally audio notes for yourself;
  • open Quick support / I Need Help resources that link to public quit and crisis information labeled by country.

Puphii’s reactions and copy are supportive feedback. They are not a measurement of physical harm, a diagnosis or medical advice. Puphii is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Quick support and crisis links are not emergency services, monitoring or clinical care.

If you’re worried about your health, talk to a healthcare professional. If you are in immediate danger or having thoughts of self-harm, contact local emergency services or a crisis line in your country.

4. Your data

Your logs, skips, plan, settings, Voice Pep Rally recordings and related entries are stored on your device. On iPhone they are designed to be excluded from iCloud and computer backups, so deleting the app deletes them, and they don’t move to a new phone with an ordinary backup restore. We don’t hold a copy of this on-device information and can’t restore it.

You can delete data in the app’s Data Management settings. The Privacy Policy describes what each option removes, and how to ask about subscription records held by our subscription provider.
